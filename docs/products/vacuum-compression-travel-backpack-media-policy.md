# Media publication policy

## Public eligibility

An asset is eligible for a public product surface only when:

- it belongs to the intended product/variant;
- rights are authorized or owned;
- verification status is verified;
- publication status is published;
- it is not restricted, a development placeholder, or concept media.

## Concept media

Concept media is permitted for internal creative development and non-transactional pre-launch work only where the surrounding UI makes its conceptual status unmistakable. It must never be used as evidence of exact-SKU verification.

## Supplier media

Supplier media may be used publicly only after commercial usage rights and exact SKU association are verified.

## Generated media

Generated imagery is concept media by default. It may define composition, lighting, environment, and mood, but must not invent product specifications, hardware, accessories, branding, certifications, or performance. After exact reference media exists, a derivative may only be approved when it is faithful to the actual SKU and rights are cleared.
