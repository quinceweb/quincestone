# Future platform architecture

Any future surface remains subject to [One Quincestone Backend](03_ONE_QUINCESTONE_BACKEND.md). A separate Supabase project requires a formally approved regulatory, regional, security, or scale architecture decision record and must not create a competing canonical source.

A protected platform can add authenticated workspaces, versioned knowledge, policy authoring, workflow adapters, consent records, role-based review queues, audit events, analytics, and environment promotion.

These capabilities are direction, not implemented claims. Public demos must remain isolated from production operational data.
